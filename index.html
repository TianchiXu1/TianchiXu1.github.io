Predictive Long-Horizon Progress Modeling with Action-Aware Quantification

Tianchi Xu*, Yalin Zheng*, Yangxin Zhu, Ruonan Ren, Zheng Fu, Yunlong Wang, Yuanxin Zhong

Robots hesitate, fail grasps, knock objects over, and recover, making progress labels based on time alone unreliable. We introduce Action-Aware Quantification to turn physical action outcomes into a unified progress standard, and train NimCritic jointly on video and progress to track non-monotonic long-horizon tasks, sharply improving both terminal success judgment and downstream robotic policy learning.

Inertial-JEPA: Efficient Object-Centric World Modeling Based on Inertial Residuals

Tianchi Xu, Yuanxin Zhong, Wenbo Zhao, Zhecheng Wang

In real videos, most frames show smooth, routine motion, while the physical interactions that truly matter for reasoning and planning are rare. Inspired by Newton's law of inertia, Inertial-JEPA measures how each object deviates from its inertial trajectory to highlight interactions and focus training on informative transitions. It matches or exceeds prior object-centric models on physical reasoning (CLEVRER) and action planning (Push-T) using about 2% of the CLEVRER training samples. On CLEVRER, training took 1.97% of the full-data run time.

Earth-JEPA: Toward Efficient and Transferable Earth-System Latent Representation Learning

Tianchi Xu, Ziqiang Ma, Zhuo Yang, Yuanjia Wei, Xiaobin Ren, Jintao Xu, Kang He, Ning Chen, Dongdong Wang

Most weather AI models spend huge compute reconstructing smooth physical grids pixel by pixel, making cross-task transfer expensive. Earth-JEPA skips pixel reconstruction and predicts future Earth states across a multi-resolution latent pyramid from heterogeneous satellite, reanalysis, and geographic inputs. It delivers strong accuracy at a fraction of the training cost and transfers across sensors and time scales, even with missing inputs.

SmoothAlpha: A Unified Physics-Conditioned Latent-Predictive Framework for Low-Visibility Image Restoration

Tianchi Xu*, Yuanpeng He*, Yuanzheng Huang, Yuanjia Wei, Wenpin Jiao, Zhi Jin

Instead of training separate restoration networks for underwater, fog, and smoke, SmoothAlpha handles all three in one compact model without needing degradation labels at test time. It pairs a smooth physical extinction estimate with clean-scene latent prediction, keeping physical interpretability while generalizing across unseen scattering environments.

Moisture-Guided Representation Learning for 3-D Precipitation Retrieval from Geostationary Infrared Observations

Tianchi Xu, Ziqiang Ma, Andrea Marinoni, Yuanpeng He, Xiaoqing Li, Chuanfeng Zhao, Kang He, Jintao Xu, Bohan Zhou, Wenbo Zhao, Haoshuang Chen, Tun Wang, Dongdong Wang, Yang Hong

Spaceborne precipitation radars see storms in 3-D, but they only pass over the same spot once every two weeks. Geostationary satellites watch continuously every 15 minutes, yet only see cloud tops. 3DPrecipNet bridges this gap by guiding latent representations with vertical water-vapor structure, enabling global 3-D precipitation monitoring at over 1,500× the sampling rate of spaceborne radar.

The Floor Is Not the Limit: Representing Physics-Aligned Weak LiDAR Returns

Tianchi Xu, Yuanpeng He, Jintao Xu, Kang He, Linyu Li, Huanyao Zhang, Dongming Jin, Bohan Zhou, Wenpin Jiao, Zhi Jin

Spaceborne lidar signals often fade into the noise floor beneath thick clouds, so standard pipelines simply clip them away. We show those weak returns still carry useful physical structure: by re-encoding them in logarithmic attenuation space (PhysicalSDC) and training on our paired dual-wavelength benchmark (AtmosBench), standard retrieval models recover hidden sub-cloud backscatter and transfer zero-shot to real satellites.
